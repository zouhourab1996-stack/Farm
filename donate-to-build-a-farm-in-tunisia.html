Donate to Help Build a Farm in Tunisia | Hedi's Campaign

Explore Hedi's personal fundraising goal of building a new life and a farm in Tunisia. Review the campaign and learn how international supporters can donate.

A farm can represent meaningful work and a long-term goal, but building one requires planning, resources and time. Hedi's personal fundraiser connects that aspiration with a broader hope for a new beginning in Tunisia.

Understand the campaign before giving

Read the original GoGetFunding page for Hedi's own description, current fundraising goal and any campaign updates. This website does not invent a fundraising target, land status, project budget or completed milestones. If a detail matters to your decision, verify it on the campaign page or ask Hedi directly.

How to support the farm dream

Use the GoGetFunding page to donate online, or contact Hedi to ask about MoneyGram and Western Union. If using a direct transfer, confirm the recipient's exact details before sending. Payment availability, fees and requirements depend on your country and provider.

Support without assumptions

A contribution supports a personal appeal; it is not an investment and does not guarantee farm ownership, income, employment or a particular result. Keep a record of your donation and review campaign updates as they become available.

Share the campaign

If you cannot donate, sharing the fundraiser with friends or community groups that welcome personal fundraising appeals may help it reach people who are interested in supporting a new beginning in Tunisia.
