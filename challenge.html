How the game works

Plant a Tree, Fly Your Flag

1 · Donate $10

Send USDT (TRC-20) — the transfer lands on the public blockchain within seconds. Wallet & QR →

2 · Claim your country

Generate your adoption certificate with your country — its flag prints right on it, next to your trees.

3 · Fly the flag

Share your certificate (the hashtag is #WorldGroveChallenge) or email us your country + transaction ID — your nation's tally climbs the wall.

4 · Watch the race

The country with the most trees when the first grove fills gets the grove's dedications written under its flag at the planting log.

How the counting stays honest: the money total and every transfer are verifiable by anyone on the public wallet ↗ — that part is mathematical. Country tallies are self-reported alongside transaction IDs (blockchains don't store nationality), so every flag on this wall traces to a real, checkable donation. No donation, no flag.
