What the public transaction list can and cannot show

Each entry shown is an incoming USDT transfer returned by the TRON data service for the project's public wallet. Open its transaction link in Tronscan to check the status, token, amount, timestamp and destination address. The wall displays up to 300 recent matching transfers; the full wallet history can be reviewed directly on Tronscan.

The project uses a planning estimate of $10 per seedling equivalent. That is a budgeting unit, not proof that a specific tree has already been planted. Actual planting, equipment purchases and other milestones are reported separately, with dated updates and supporting records on the transparency page. Donor names are not stored on-chain, so the wall displays transaction data rather than private identities.

How to verify a donation record

Open the transaction link in Tronscan and check that the transfer succeeded, the token is USDT on TRC-20, the receiving address matches the project's public wallet, and the amount and timestamp match the wall entry. A wallet address identifies where funds were sent; it does not reveal a donor's name. A confirmed transfer proves a contribution was received, not that a specific seedling has already been planted or assigned.
