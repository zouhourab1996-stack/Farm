What the public grove wall proves

Each entry is a USDT transfer visible on the public TRON ledger. You can open the transaction reference and independently check its amount, timestamp and destination wallet. The wall reports contributions received; it does not claim that every funded seedling has already been planted.

The project uses a planning estimate of $10 per seedling equivalent. Actual planting, equipment purchases and other milestones are reported separately, with dated updates and supporting records on the transparency page. Donor names are not stored on-chain, so the wall displays transaction data rather than private identities.
