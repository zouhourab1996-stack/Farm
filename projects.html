The master plan

How 16 Hectares Grow, Step by Step

Small farms don't fail for lack of ambition — they fail for trying everything at once. This project deliberately does the opposite: each phase must deliver before the next one asks for a single dollar, and the funding for all of them is visible on the same blockchain address, forever.

The first cooperative grove on the land's best-suited blocks: adopted trees at $10 a seedling, drip irrigation, organic practice, and training days for the local community. Goal: $5,000, tracked live on-chain.

The land, in plain words

Sixteen hectares — about 22 football pitches — of rural Tunisian farmland, already owned by the founder. No donation money buys land, pays salaries, or covers "administration": that's a written commitment on this site. The exact location and photos of the plot are shared with donors first and published with the first planting log, for the family's safety and privacy until the project has a constant presence on the ground.
