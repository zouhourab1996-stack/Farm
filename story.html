The personal story

Hedi's Story: From Addiction Toward a New Life in Tunisia

Hedi's campaign is built around a simple hope: to leave a difficult chapter behind and work toward a stable life with family and a farm in Tunisia.

The campaign title speaks openly about the challenge of addiction and the wish to build something different. Recovery and rebuilding a life are not instant events. They take time, consistent effort, support and practical resources. Hedi is asking people who feel able to help him take steps toward that future.

Why the dream includes a family and a farm

The vision is not only about getting through a difficult period. It is also about creating a more grounded future: family life, meaningful work and the possibility of developing a farm in Tunisia. The countryside image on this page illustrates that hope; it should not be mistaken for proof that a particular property has already been acquired or developed.

How to support the campaign

Read the full GoGetFunding campaign for Hedi's own description and current details. You can donate through the platform or contact Hedi to ask about MoneyGram or Western Union. Confirm recipient details privately before sending funds.
