Donate to Help a Family in Tunisia | Hedi's Fundraiser

Looking for a way to help a family in Tunisia? Learn how to review Hedi's personal fundraiser and donate through GoGetFunding or verified direct-transfer instructions.

People searching for ways to help a family in Tunisia often want a clear, trustworthy route to contribute. This guide introduces one personal fundraiser and explains how to review it before making a decision.

Start with the original campaign

Hedi's fundraiser describes a personal effort to move beyond addiction and work toward a stable future that includes family life and a farm in Tunisia. The original campaign page is the primary source for the full description and current fundraising information. Read it carefully so you understand the request before donating.

Choose a donation method that you can verify

Supporters can use the GoGetFunding campaign page or contact Hedi to ask about MoneyGram and Western Union. Direct-transfer requirements vary by country. Before paying, confirm the recipient's exact legal name and the transfer instructions privately. Keep your receipt and share any transfer reference only through a private channel.

Give with realistic expectations

A personal donation can provide practical support, but it cannot guarantee a particular outcome. This fundraiser is not presented as a registered charity, and no tax deduction is claimed. A transfer receipt confirms that money was sent; it does not independently verify how funds were later used.

How to take the next step

If you want to help, visit the original campaign page first. If you have questions about direct transfer methods, message Hedi before sending anything. You can also share the campaign with someone who may be interested in supporting a personal new beginning.
