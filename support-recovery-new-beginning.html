Support a New Beginning After Addiction | Hedi's Campaign

Learn how to support a personal fundraiser focused on a fresh start after addiction, family stability and a long-term farm dream in Tunisia.

When someone asks for support while trying to move beyond addiction, it is important to respond with dignity and realistic expectations. Recovery is personal and ongoing; it should never be reduced to a dramatic promise or a single donation.

What this personal campaign is about

Hedi's fundraiser describes a wish to leave addiction behind and work toward family stability and a farm in Tunisia. The campaign is a personal appeal for help with that next chapter. The original fundraising page should be treated as the primary source for Hedi's own account and any updates.

Ways supporters can help

Those who choose to contribute can donate through GoGetFunding or contact Hedi to ask about MoneyGram and Western Union. Direct transfers should only be made after confirming the recipient information privately. If you send money, keep your receipt and do not share reference numbers in public posts.

Respect privacy and recovery

This website does not publish private medical details or make claims about treatment, diagnoses or recovery milestones. Support should be offered without stigma, pressure or assumptions. A contribution is voluntary support for a personal campaign; it cannot guarantee a particular health, family or financial result.

Review before donating

Read the original campaign details, decide whether the request aligns with your wishes and use a payment route you understand. This is not a registered charity and no tax deductibility is claimed.
