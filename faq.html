Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about donating, verifying, and what happens after — answered plainly.

How do I donate?

Copy the TRON wallet address from the home page, open a wallet that supports USDT on the TRC-20 network, and check the address and network carefully before confirming. The project's planning estimate treats each $10 as one seedling equivalent; it is a budget unit, not proof of planting. After the transfer is confirmed, you can verify it on the public chain. The grove wall displays it after the data provider has indexed it, so there can be a delay.

Which network — TRC-20, ERC-20 or BEP-20?

TRC-20 only. Network fees vary by wallet and current conditions, so check the fee shown by your wallet before confirming. Funds sent on another network or to the wrong address may be unrecoverable; this is why the warning sits beside the project address.

Why USDT instead of PayPal or a card?

This project currently accepts USDT on TRC-20 and does not offer card checkout. A public transaction record lets visitors verify transfers to the project wallet, but it is not a substitute for reviewing the published budget, dated updates and receipts. Payment options and availability vary by country and provider.

How do I know the money actually plants trees?

Transfers into and out of the project's public wallet can be inspected on Tronscan. That proves wallet activity, not the final use of funds or that a specific seedling has been planted. The published budget allocates 45% to seedlings and planting, 30% to drip irrigation, 15% to tools and 10% to farmer training; the land is already owned. The transparency page is where dated photos, receipts and milestone updates should be checked.

What do I get when I donate?

A permanent on-chain receipt, an entry on the public grove wall, and a printable personalized adoption certificate you generate yourself — tree count included. It makes a genuinely unusual gift for a gardener or an olive-oil lover.

Is this a registered charity? Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. This is an independent personal initiative by Anis Touati in Tunisia, not a registered charity or NGO, and contributions are not tax-deductible. The project publishes a public wallet and transaction records, alongside a separate transparency page for budget and milestone evidence.

Does a transfer prove that a tree has been planted?

No. A confirmed transfer proves that funds reached the wallet. Planting is a separate physical milestone and should be supported by dated photos, receipts and updates on the transparency page.

Does the Grove Wall show the complete wallet history?

The wall displays up to 300 recent matching incoming USDT transfers returned by the data provider. For the full wallet history, open the public address in Tronscan. Data-provider indexing can lag behind the blockchain.

Can I help without donating crypto?

Yes — sharing the project with one person who loves olive oil or gardening helps more than you'd think. Questions, feedback and local offers of help are welcome at anistouati74@gmail.com.
