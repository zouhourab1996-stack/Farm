Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about donating, verifying, and what happens after — answered plainly.

How do I donate?

Copy our TRON wallet address (the button on the home page does it in one click), open any USDT-capable wallet — Binance, Trust Wallet, TronLink — send USDT on the TRC-20 network and choose your amount ($10 funds one seedling). Your transfer appears on the public blockchain within seconds, and on the grove wall the next time the page loads.

Which network — TRC-20, ERC-20 or BEP-20?

TRC-20 only. Fees run about one dollar, versus $5–15 on Ethereum. Funds sent on any other network cannot be recovered — this is the single most important detail of crypto donations, which is why the warning box sits next to our address.

Why USDT instead of PayPal or a card?

PayPal cannot receive payments in Tunisia — the platform doesn't support receiving for Tunisian accounts, so a card checkout was never an option for this project. Crypto sidesteps that entirely, and as a bonus every donation lands on a public ledger anyone can audit — stronger transparency than any payment processor offers.

How do I know the money actually plants trees?

The wallet is public and permanent: every transfer in and out is visible on Tronscan forever, so spending can't hide. The budget is published (45% seedlings & planting · 30% drip irrigation · 15% tools · 10% training — the land itself is already owned, so no donation ever buys land), no salaries are taken, and the transparency page commits to dated photo documentation of the site, planting, and receipts as milestones are reached.

What do I get when I donate?

A permanent on-chain receipt, an entry on the public grove wall, and a printable personalized adoption certificate you generate yourself — tree count included. It makes a genuinely unusual gift for a gardener or an olive-oil lover.

Is this a registered charity? Is my donation tax-deductible?

No — and we say so plainly rather than dressing it up. This is an independent personal initiative by Anis Touati in Tunisia, not a registered charity or NGO; donations are not tax-deductible. What it offers instead of institutional status is radical transparency: a public wallet and an unforgeable on-chain record of every donation.

Can I help without donating crypto?

Yes — sharing the project with one person who loves olive oil or gardening helps more than you'd think. Questions, feedback and local offers of help are welcome at anistouati74@gmail.com.
