Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this fundraiser for?

This personal fundraiser supports Hedi's stated goal of moving beyond addiction and working toward a stable new life, family and a farm in Tunisia. Read the original campaign for his full description.

How can I donate internationally?

Use the GoGetFunding campaign page or contact Hedi to ask whether MoneyGram or Western Union is available in your country.

Can I send a direct transfer?

Contact Hedi first for the exact recipient details. Confirm them privately before sending.

Where should I send the transfer reference?

Send it privately to Hedi through WhatsApp. Do not post MTCNs or transfer references publicly.

Is this a registered charity?

No. It is a personal fundraiser and does not claim tax deductibility.

Does a donation guarantee a particular result?

No. A donation supports the stated campaign goals but cannot guarantee recovery, family outcomes, land purchase or farm development.
