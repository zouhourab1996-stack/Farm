How to Donate to Hedi's Fundraiser in Tunisia

Supporters around the world can start with the GoGetFunding campaign page. If you prefer a direct transfer, contact Hedi first to confirm the correct recipient details.

1. Donate through GoGetFunding

Visit the original campaign, read the fundraiser information and use the donation options displayed there.

Donate through GoGetFunding

2. Send through MoneyGram

Availability and requirements vary by country. Message Hedi to request the exact recipient name and instructions. Check that the details match the information supplied to you before paying.

Request MoneyGram details

3. Send through Western Union

Contact Hedi before initiating a transfer, confirm the recipient information carefully and keep your receipt. Once complete, share the reference number privately so the transfer can be identified.

Request Western Union details
Protect your transfer: Never send money to unconfirmed recipient details. Do not post MTCNs, reference numbers, identity documents or other sensitive information publicly. Share transfer references only in a private WhatsApp message.

Is this a registered charity?

No. This is an independent personal fundraiser. It does not claim registered-charity status or tax deductibility. A contribution is support for a personal appeal, not an investment and not a guarantee of a specific outcome.
