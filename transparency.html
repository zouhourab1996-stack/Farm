Most donation pages show you a number they typed. We show you the blockchain. Here is every way to check this project without taking our word for anything.

2. The counters on this site read the chain directly

The totals on the home page and grove wall are fetched in your browser from TronGrid — the official public TRON API — at the moment you load the page. We never store or serve a total ourselves. If the API is unreachable, the pages say so instead of showing a stale number.
