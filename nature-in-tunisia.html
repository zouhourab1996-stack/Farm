Why this website exists

Olive Grove Tunisia shares accessible information about rural landscapes and nature-conscious farming. It also hosts a clearly identified personal fundraiser by Hedi, whose long-term hope is to build a more stable life around family and a farm in Tunisia. The fundraising campaign is a personal appeal—not an established farm, conservation organization or registered charity.
